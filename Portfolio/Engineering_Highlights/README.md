# Engineering Highlights — Electronic Systems & Product Engineering

[**View the approved 16-page portfolio — Version 29 (PDF)**](PCB_IoT_Solutions_Embedded_Engineering_Portfolio.pdf)

This portfolio brings together commercial electronic product development and delivery, embedded systems and recent hardware design work. It leads with product engineering across development, verification, NPI, manufacturing and deployment, supported by selected project evidence.

## Commercial product engineering

- **FAAC:** RF hardware development, NPI and volume manufacturing, with production engineering and engineering leadership.
- **Watt Footprint:** energy monitoring hardware and embedded firmware, including delivery of 25 pilot units.
- **AmbiSense:** low-power optimization and sensing for connected electronic products.

## Recent hardware and embedded projects

STM32WL LoRa, LAN7800 USB 3.0 to Gigabit Ethernet, Nordic nRF52840 BLE, ESP32-S3 wireless sensing and EdgeLink cellular/GNSS projects demonstrate schematic and PCB design, embedded development and engineering documentation. The portfolio includes simulation, verification, DFM/DFT and manufacturing preparation, with the scope of the evidence identified for each project.

The STM32WL, LAN7800 and EdgeLink boards are CAD design projects, without claims of manufacture or physical board validation. Nordic and ESP32-S3 firmware demonstrations use separate development hardware. Commercial manufacturing experience, pilot delivery, simulation and physical demonstrations are presented as distinct evidence.
