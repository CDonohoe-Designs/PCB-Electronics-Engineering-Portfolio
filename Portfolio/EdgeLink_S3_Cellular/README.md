# EdgeLink S3 Cellular Hardware Engineering

**Hardware design · ESP32-S3 · LTE-M / NB-IoT · GNSS · Power management · KiCad · Manufacturing**

[**View the EdgeLink S3 Cellular Engineering Portfolio (PDF)**](EdgeLink_S3_Cellular_Engineering_Portfolio.pdf)

## Overview

This portfolio documents the EdgeLink S3 Cellular Rev A hardware design, integrating an ESP32-S3 processor, SIM7080G cellular/GNSS modem, battery and USB power circuitry, and sensor interfaces on a compact four-layer KiCad PCB.

## Hardware and PCB engineering

- ESP32-S3-MINI-1-N8 processing and SIM7080G cellular/GNSS integration.
- USB-C interface, BQ25185 battery charging, TPS63802 power conversion and MAX17048 fuel gauging.
- BMI323 motion sensing, SHT40 humidity/temperature sensing and BMP384 pressure sensing.
- Four-layer, 56 × 52 mm PCB design with schematic partitioning, placement and routing.
- Interfaces, power architecture and manufacturing documentation developed for the Rev A design.

## Manufacturing preparation

- Fabrication and assembly handoff material, including BOM, component placement and JLCPCB-oriented outputs.
- Design documentation covering the hardware architecture and implementation choices.

## Engineering evidence

The PDF presents the Rev A system architecture, schematic and four-layer PCB design, component selection, power and communications integration, and fabrication/assembly documentation.
